Universal Hitting & Pitching Principles Across Counts

1 Never Concede Pitch 1

The First Pitch is a Hitter's Count

First-pitch batting average (.334) matches a 2-0 count. Taking a fastball directly down the pipe on 0-0 sacrifices the best driving pitch of the plate appearance and forces you into a 0-1 pitcher's duel.

2 The 1-1 Inflection Point

The Fork in the Road

A 1-1 count determines the fate of the at-bat. Winning the pitch pushes you to 2-1 (.332 AVG); losing it drops you straight to 1-2 (.164 AVG). Pitchers throw offspeed at 52% rates here to induce weak contact.

3 Two-Strike Approach

Choke Up, Battle, Spoil

Hitters should widen their base 2 inches, choke up 1 inch, and shorten their stride. Do not try to hit home runs; fight off tough spin with fouls and poke anything outer-half into the opposite field.
